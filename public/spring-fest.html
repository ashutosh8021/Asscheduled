01The fest that has been running since 1960IIT Kharagpur · West Bengal

Five days, true spirit of youth

Spring Fest is IIT Kharagpur’s annual social and cultural festival, and we are the Official Trip Partner for 2027

It has run since 1960 under one line, True Spirit of Youth, past editions have put on more than 130 events and drawn a footfall of over 80,000 in 2019, you get yourself to Howrah or Kharagpur station and everything after that is handled

04The campusIndia’s first IIT

Your address for four nights

You are not commuting to this fest, you are staying inside it

First IIT, opened1951
Where classes beganHijli

Classes began in the old Hijli Detention Camp building, now Shaheed Bhawan, which houses the Nehru Museum of Science and Technology. Spring Fest 2027 lands inside IIT Kharagpur’s 75th-year celebrations, which run from August 2025 to August 2027

05Then the seaPlan 02 · two days by road

Then the sea

Off campus at 8:00 in the morning on the 25th, New and Old Digha, then Tajpur for the casuarina trees and the red crabs, then a beach resort at Mandarmani for the night

The 26th is one more walk on the sand, breakfast, and the road back to Kolkata, we aim to have you at Howrah Junction around 4:30 PM, book your train home for after 7, the 26th is Republic Day and the road decides

If you cancel · Spring Fest 2027
30+ days before75%
15 – 29 days before50%

Days are counted back from 21 January 2027, the day of pickup. The percentages apply to the refundable portion, after non-refundable costs such as entry tickets are taken out. No show, no refund.
