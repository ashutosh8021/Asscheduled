Terms of travel

Eligibility & ID

You must be 18 or over. Government ID plus college ID or degree are checked at boarding. Details that don’t match your booking void the seat, with no refund.

Conduct

The trip captain’s call is final on the ground. Behaviour that endangers the group, yours or anyone’s safety, ends your trip on the spot, at your own cost home. Illegal substances end it faster.

Schedule

Published itineraries are the plan; weather, fests and India get votes too. Equivalent substitutions may occur. Evening plans can stay sealed until departure by design.

Liability

We run verified stays, licensed transport and a written emergency protocol, and carry a first aid kit plus a charged phone. Personal belongings remain your responsibility. Travel insurance is recommended.

Payments

A booking amount confirms your seat and the balance is due before departure, as shown on each departure page and in the booking form. Payments are made to the AS SCHEDULED payment QR shown on this website.

Summary only. The terms shared with your booking confirmation govern.
