FILE 07 — FREQUENTLY ASKED

Why apply instead of book? +

Nineteen seats only work if the nineteen fit together. Booking fills buses; selection builds trips. We read every application, pick the group, and give no reasons for rejections. No appeals either. It keeps things honest.

What happens to my ₹500? +

Not selected: automatic refund to your payment source within 5–7 working days via Razorpay. Selected: the ₹500 adjusts against your trip fee. The fee exists so only people who mean it apply. It's a filter, not revenue.

What's actually included? +

Everything that matters: intercity travel from your boarding hub, both ways. Fest tickets. Five nights verified twin-share stay. All meals, three a day. Every transfer. Director plus Trip Captain on ground, first-aid and SOS protocol. You bring yourself, a bag, and ID. Full lists sit inside each case file on the board.

How do I pay if selected? +

48-hour window after your selection email. Full payment or 50/50 split — second half due 21 days before departure. UPI, cards, netbanking via Razorpay. Miss the window and the seat moves to the waitlist. We keep schedules; it's in the name.

I'd be coming alone. Weird? +

Most of the nineteen come solo. That's the design. If you already had the group, you wouldn't need us.

Who's actually running this on ground? +

Every departure carries the Director and one dedicated Trip Captain — nineteen travellers, two crew. Verified stays, a written emergency protocol, guardian contact on file for every seat, and a captain whose entire job is making the schedule survive contact with reality.

Who can apply? +

18–26. College students or recent graduates. Any college, any city, any course. A valid college ID or degree helps your file; a good answer to question four helps it more.

What if I drop out after selection? +

Trip fee refunds by slab: more than 30 days out, 80%. Between 15 and 30 days, 50%. Under 15 days, nothing — the seat's already yours and empty. Full slabs live in the Refund Policy, footer, Paperwork.

Paperwork

Application fee — ₹500

Fully refundable. Not selected: automatic refund to your original payment method within 5–7 working days, processed via Razorpay. Selected: the ₹500 adjusts against your trip fee. No action needed from you in either case.

Trip fee — after selection

  • Cancellation more than 30 days before departure: 80% refund.
  • 15 to 30 days before departure: 50% refund.
  • Under 15 days: no refund — your seat is held and cannot be resold in time.
  • Departure cancelled by us for any reason: 100% refund, including the ₹500, within 7 working days.

Fest force majeure

If a fest cancels or shifts dates, the departure shifts with it. You choose: move with the new dates, transfer to any open departure, or take a full refund.

Refund timelines depend on your bank once Razorpay releases funds. Summary only — the signed terms at payment govern.

Eligibility & ID

18–26 years. Government ID plus college ID or degree checked at boarding. Details that don't match your application void the seat, no refund.

Conduct

The Trip Captain's call is final on the ground. Behaviour that endangers the group — yours or anyone's safety — ends your trip on the spot, at your own cost home. Illegal substances end it faster.

Schedule

Published itineraries are the plan; weather, fests and India get votes too. Equivalent substitutions may occur. Evening plans stay sealed until departure by design.

Liability

We run verified stays, licensed transport, a written emergency protocol and carry a first-aid kit plus a charged phone. Personal belongings remain your responsibility. Travel insurance is offered at payment; take it.

Summary only — the signed terms issued with your selection email govern.

What we collect

What you type into Form 7A: particulars, contact details, nine answers, one photo. Payment runs entirely inside Razorpay — card and UPI details never touch our servers.

What we do with it

Selection. Emergency contact during the trip. Nothing else. No sale, no sharing, no ad targeting, no newsletter you didn't ask for.

Retention

Rejected applications: deleted within 90 days of refund. Selected travellers: kept for the season for operations and legal compliance, then archived.

Your rights

Write to applications@asscheduled.in for a copy or deletion of your data. We answer within 7 working days.
